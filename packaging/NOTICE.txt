YTFast
======

YTFast plays the audio of YouTube videos. It does not include yt-dlp: install it
separately (https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) and make sure it is on PATH, or set
its location in Settings. Use it in line with YouTube's terms of service and the
copyright rules where you live.
